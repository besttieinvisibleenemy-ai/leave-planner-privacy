Privacy Policy

Leave Planner · Effective date: 12 November 2026

Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Leave Planner (package name com.sarath.leaveplanner), developed by Sarath Kumar B (“we”, “us”). Leave Planner helps you plan personal, casual, sick and earned leave, government holidays, week offs, notes, alarms and clocks on your own phone.

A short summary

Leave Planner stores your profile, leave records, holidays, notes, alarms and settings only on your device. The app has no user account and no server of its own. It does not collect, sell or share your data, and it does not include analytics or advertising. The app is built without the Android Internet permission, so it cannot send your information over the network.

Data stored on your device

When you use Leave Planner, the following information stays on your phone, in the app’s private storage:

  • Your profile (for example your name, designation, date of birth, joining date, contract details and week-off day).
  • Leave balances and leave records, including any half-day choices and medical-certificate flags you enter.
  • Government holidays and other dates you add.
  • Notes, note reminders, alarms, clock and time-zone settings, and other app preferences.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes this information from the device.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not operate a Leave Planner server or cloud service.
  • We do not collect, receive or have access to the information you enter in the app.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and the app contains no ads.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Backups you choose to make

You can export or import a JSON backup of your Leave Planner data when you choose to:

  • Save a backup: the app can write a JSON file to the Download/LeavePlanner folder on your device, using Android’s MediaStore. The app does not request broad storage or photo-library access.
  • Share a backup: you can share the JSON file through Android’s share sheet (for example to Files, Drive or another app you pick). The file leaves the app only because you chose a destination.
  • Import a backup: you pick a file with the system file picker. The app reads only the file you select, and does not browse the rest of your storage.

Once you share or save a backup outside the app, that copy is under your control and under the privacy policy of the app or service you sent it to.

Android device backup

Leave Planner itself does not upload your data to us. If you have Android backup or device-to-device transfer turned on, Android may include some of this app’s settings (information stored as SharedPreferences, such as alarm and clock settings) in your device backup or transfer. On some older Android versions, other app files may also be included. That backup is held under Google’s or your device maker’s backup policy, not by us, and we do not receive it. You can turn device backup off in your phone’s system settings.

Permissions

Leave Planner asks only for the permissions needed to run reminders, alarms and the clock features on your device:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used to show leave-lapse reminders, note reminders, optional Thirukural-of-the-day notices, alarm alerts and a clock-mode shortcut. You can refuse or later disable notifications in system settings; reminders and alarms will then not appear as notifications.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): used so leave reminders, note reminders, the optional daily Kural notice and clock alarms can ring at the time you set, rather than at a later approximate time.
  • Full-screen alarms (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): used so an alarm can appear full screen and wake the device when it is locked, like a clock alarm.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE): used so alarms and reminders can vibrate as well as play a sound.
  • Boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): after the phone restarts, is updated, or after a time or time-zone change, the app restores your scheduled reminders and alarms. Android does not keep those schedules across a reboot on its own.
  • Wake lock / keep the screen on (WAKE_LOCK, and the keep-screen-on window flag): used so clock mode and the alarm screen can stay awake and visible while you are using them. The app does not run a background wake lock to collect data.
  • Calendar (insert only): when you choose to add a reminder to your calendar, Leave Planner opens your calendar app with a pre-filled event (an insert intent). It does not request calendar permission and does not read, edit or delete events already in your calendar. Any event you save is stored by the calendar app you confirm it in.
  • File access for backup: the app does not declare storage, photos or media permissions. Export uses MediaStore or the share sheet, and import uses the system file picker, as described above. A private FileProvider is used only to hand the backup file you chose to share to the app you pick.

The app also provides an optional home-screen clock widget and a charging screen saver. Those features display the clocks you configured; they do not send data off the device.

Bundled content

The Thirukural of the day (Tamil verses and Tamil and English explanations) and the central government holiday lists are packaged inside the app and used offline. The app does not download this content from the internet.

Children

Leave Planner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page and change the effective date at the top. The updated policy applies from that date.
